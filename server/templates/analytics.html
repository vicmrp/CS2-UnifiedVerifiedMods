OPTIONAL WEBSITE ANALYTICS

Someone was here :-)

I make UVM and Observe as hobby projects. A little feedback about how many people visit helps me understand whether the website is useful.

What does “someone” mean?

If you choose Allow analytics, Google Analytics records visits and basic device information. I can see reports such as page views, approximate country, browser and device type. It does not tell me your name, GitHub account or which person you are.

This is not a promise of complete anonymity: Google receives connection information, including your IP address, and analytics cookies can distinguish visits. See Google’s privacy policy ↗ for its data handling.

Your mod activity stays out of analytics.

UVM does not send mod files, file hashes, search text, URL query strings, signing keys or Observe activity to Google Analytics. These analytics are about visits to this website, not monitoring your game.

Your choice.

No thanks leaves analytics off. UVM and Observe work without it. Your choice is saved in this browser. Use Analytics preferences in the website footer to change it later; withdrawing consent stops future collection in this browser and removes its accessible analytics cookies. It does not erase information already sent to Google.

This explanation page does not load analytics.
